[Massimiliano Pontil](/people/massimiliano-pontil/) is recruiting several postdocs for LEO, which studies learning linear evolution operators from data, with applications to molecular dynamics and climate modelling. Researchers in data-driven dynamical systems, machine learning theory, or computational linear algebra are encouraged to apply; see the [blog posts](/blog/) by [Vladimir R. Kostić](/people/vladimir-kostic/) and [Pietro Novelli](/people/pietro-novelli/) and the [careers page](/careers/) for how to apply.
