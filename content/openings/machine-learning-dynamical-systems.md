[Massimiliano Pontil](/people/massimiliano-pontil/) has multiple postdoctoral positions available on the recently funded ERC Advanced Grant project **LEO – Learning Evolution Operators**. Collaboration contracts for up to three years, based at CHT@Erzelli, Genoa.

The project studies how to learn linear evolution operators from data and use them for statistical inference. A main focus is learning efficiently via interactive perturbations and transferring information across systems in a multitask setting. Applications include molecular dynamics and climate modelling, in collaboration with leading experts in these fields.

We are particularly keen to hear from researchers in:

1. data-driven dynamical systems, SDEs and PDEs;
2. mathematical statistics and machine learning theory; or
3. numerical and computational linear algebra.

For a flavour of the work, see [Massimiliano Pontil's recent papers](/people/massimiliano-pontil/) and the [blog posts](/blog/) by [Vladimir R. Kostić](/people/vladimir-kostic/) and [Pietro Novelli](/people/pietro-novelli/).
