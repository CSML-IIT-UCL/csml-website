Arya Akhavan completed a PhD in theoretical machine learning at ENSAE, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, and IIT from 2019 to 2023, advised by Alexandre Tsybakov and Massimiliano Pontil, followed by a postdoctoral position at École Polytechnique until 2024 working with Karim Lounici and El Mahdi El Mhamdi. Arya is now a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oxford, working with Patrick Rebeschini.
