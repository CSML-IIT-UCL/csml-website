Pedro Juan Buigues Jorro is a computational biophysicist who was a postdoctoral researcher at IIT from June 2023 to May 2026, working between the CSML group and the Atomistic Simulations group. Pedro completed a PhD in computational biophysics in the Rosta group at University College London, developing machine-learning-enhanced sampling methods for molecular dynamics.
