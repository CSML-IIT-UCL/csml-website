Jordan Patracone (formerly Jordan Frecon) was a postdoctoral researcher in the CSML group at IIT from 2017 to 2020, supervised by Massimiliano Pontil and working on non-smooth bilevel optimization for multi-task learning, and an external collaborator of the group in 2021. Jordan is now an associate professor at Télécom Saint-Etienne and a member of the MALICE Inria team at Laboratoire Hubert Curien.
