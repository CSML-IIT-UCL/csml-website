Timothée Devergne was a joint postdoctoral researcher in the CSML group and the Atomistic Simulations group at IIT from November 2023 to August 2026, after a PhD at Sorbonne Université. The work at IIT applied machine learning to molecular dynamics and enhanced sampling. Timothée is now an assistant professor (Maître de conférences) at the Université de Lille, in the UMET laboratory.
